The wobble

Why “true” and “mean” differ

The sun doesn't keep perfect time. Earth's tilted, slightly elliptical orbit pushes the real noon up to 16 minutes either side of the average. Mean time smooths it out; true time follows the sky.

What the clock needs

One number: longitude

Longitude
Required. 15° is one hour; 1° is four minutes. Mean solar time is UTC plus longitude ÷ 15.
Date
For true time only. The equation of time depends on the day of the year.
Latitude
Optional. Does not move noon. Used here for sunrise, sunset and the chart.
Elevation
Ignored. Standing on a mountain shifts sunrise by seconds and noon by nothing.

Equation of time is accurate to about 30 seconds; sunrise and sunset to a couple of minutes (atmospheric refraction is a fixed 0.833°). Good for a clock, not for navigation. Your location never leaves this page.
